# Legal and fairness constraints

Price optimization is one of the few marketing services where the mathematically optimal answer can
be unlawful. Check this **before** the number is quoted, not after it is challenged.

## Exposure classes

| Class | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Price discrimination law** | Differential pricing to competing trade customers without a cost or availability justification |
| **Consumer protection** | Misleading reference prices ("was/now"), drip pricing, undisclosed personalization |
| **Essential goods and emergencies** | Anti-gouging rules on necessities during declared events — the exact case where a scarcity model recommends a rise |
| **Protected characteristics** | Any price that correlates with a protected class, including through a proxy |
| **Algorithmic collusion** | Shared pricing software or signalling that aligns competitor prices, even without an agreement |
| **Sector rules** | Utilities, pharma, insurance, credit — statutory caps and rate approval |
| **Resale price maintenance** | Dictating a reseller's price, unlawful in many jurisdictions |

## The fairness test

Before shipping a differentiated price, answer three questions:

1. **Can the customer choose their way into the lower price?** A fence they can cross by choosing a
   pack, a channel, a time or a tier is defensible. A price set by what a model infers about them is
   not.
2. **Would this survive publication?** If the mechanism on a front page would embarrass the brand,
   the margin is borrowed against reputation.
3. **Is the differentiator a proxy?** Postcode, device type and browsing history correlate with
   protected characteristics. "We never used the protected attribute" is not a defence if the proxy
   reproduces the effect.

## What may safely carry a price difference

Version, pack size, quantity tier, channel, timing, service level, contract commitment, verified
trade or student status, genuine cost-to-serve differences.

## What should not

Inferred income, inferred urgency, device, postcode standing in for demographics, or any per-person
price for an identical good and identical service.

## Documentation

State the constraint that bound, and the margin given up to respect it. A number that was capped by
a fairness or legal limit should say so in the report — that is not a weaker answer, it is the
defensible one.

**This is an engineering checklist, not legal advice.** Jurisdictions differ; route anything novel
to counsel before launch.
